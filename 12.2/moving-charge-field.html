Model used for the relative indicator: low-speed point-charge field ∝ |q|v sinφ / r², where φ is between v and the displacement to P. This formula is enrichment (not on the AP Physics 2 equation sheet). The lesson requires qualitative comparison, not numerical use of this formula.

For the moving charge: B is perpendicular to both v and r. At P directly ahead of or behind the charge, B = 0 in this model.
