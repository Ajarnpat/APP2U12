What am I seeing?

Field lines are mathematical visualizations, not physical strands. The displayed number of lines is illustrative, not a calibrated field strength. Circular-wire and coil fields are derived from a numerical Biot–Savart model; the bar magnet is shown using an ideal dipole approximation. A slowly moving point charge is shown using the nonrelativistic instantaneous-field approximation. Some field lines extend beyond the drawing window, but continue as closed loops. Crossings in a projected 3D view do not mean physical field lines intersect. These models help visualize field geometry; they are not a replacement for the AP Physics 2 quantitative equations sheet.
